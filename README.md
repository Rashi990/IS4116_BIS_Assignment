- ⭐ **Average Rating** – Average rating given by the customer

- 😊 **Satisfaction Level** – Satisfied, Neutral, Unsatisfied

## 🔹 Customer Segmentation Using K-Means  

The K-Means clustering groups customers into **three distinct clusters** based on their spending and purchasing behavior.  

| Cluster  | Characteristics                     | Business Strategy                                       |
|----------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **Cluster 0** | Low spending, few purchases     | Offer promotions, encourage repeat purchases        |
| **Cluster 1** | Medium spending, average purchases | Provide loyalty programs, personalized discounts    |
| **Cluster 2** | High spending, frequent purchases | Premium membership, exclusive deals                 |

---

## 📊 Interpretation & Business Implications  

✔ **High-value customers drive revenue** – Retain Cluster 2 customers with premium services, VIP programs, and exclusive deals.  
✔ **Total Spend & Items Purchased are key indicators** – Increase basket size with bundles, bulk discounts, and personalized recommendations.  
✔ **Low-spending customers need engagement** – Boost retention with promotions, loyalty programs, and referral incentives.  
✔ **Medium spenders offer upselling opportunities** – Encourage spending through subscription-based services and targeted promotions.  
✔ **Personalization is crucial for retention** – Leverage AI-driven recommendations, targeted ads, and personalized communication.
